Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish that consists of layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 (6-ounce) can tomato paste
  • 1 (15-ounce) can tomato sauce
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil leaves
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 1/2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 12 lasagna noodles

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large skillet, cook ground beef, onion, and garlic over medium heat until meat is no longer pink; drain excess fat.
  3. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, tomato sauce, sugar, basil, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. In a separate bowl, combine ricotta cheese, 2 cups of mozzarella cheese, and Parmesan cheese.
  5. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions; drain and rinse with cold water
  6. In a 9x13-inch baking dish, spread 1 cup of meat sauce on the bottom. Layer with 4 noodles, 1/3 of the ricotta cheese mixture, 1/3 of the remaining meat sauce, and 1 cup of mozzarella cheese. Repeat layers twice, ending with meat sauce and remaining mozzarella cheese on top.
  7. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes, or until cheese is melted and bubbly. Let stand for 10 minutes before serving.
