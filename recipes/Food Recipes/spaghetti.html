Spaghetti

Spaghetti is a classic Italian pasta dish that is simple yet delicious. It is a staple food in many households and is often served with a variety of sauces.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound spaghetti
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions; drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add minced garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add cooked spaghetti to the skillet and toss to coat with the garlic and olive oil.
  4. Season with dried oregano, salt, and black pepper. Toss well to combine.
  5. Serve hot, topped with grated Parmesan cheese. Enjoy your delicious spaghetti!
