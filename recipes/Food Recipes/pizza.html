Pizza

Pizza is a beloved dish that consists of a flattened round base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and various other ingredients.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 packet active dry yeast
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/2 cup marinara sauce
  • 8 ounces mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, dissolve the yeast in warm water. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes until frothy.
  2. Add the sugar, olive oil, flour, salt, and oregano to the yeast mixture. Mix until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let it rise in a warm place for 1 hour or until doubled in size.
  5. Punch down the dough and roll it out on a floured surface to form a circle.
  6. Transfer the rolled-out dough to a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  7. Spread the marinara sauce evenly over the dough.
  8. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the sauce. < li>Bake in a preheated oven at 425°F for 15-20 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
  9. Remove from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving. Enjoy your homemade pizza!
