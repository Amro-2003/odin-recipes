Salad

A salad is a dish consisting of a mixture of mixed greens and other ingredients, typically with a dressing.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup cucumber
  • 1/4 cup red onion
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice

Instructions:

  1. Wash and dry the mixed greens.
  2. Chop the cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion.
  3. In a large bowl, combine the greens and chopped vegetables.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil and lemon juice to make the dressing.
  5. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss to combine.
