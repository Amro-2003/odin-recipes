Smoothie

A smoothie is a creamy, refreshing drink made with fruits, vegetables, and other ingredients blended together.

Ingredients:

  • 1 banana
  • 1 cup frozen berries
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon honey

Instructions:

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Pour into a glass and enjoy!
