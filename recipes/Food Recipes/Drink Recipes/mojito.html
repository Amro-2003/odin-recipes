Mojito

A mojito is a traditional Cuban cocktail that is perfect for warm weather. It's a refreshing blend of rum, mint, lime juice, and soda water.

Ingredients:

  • 2 ounces white rum
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 6-8 fresh mint leaves
  • 1 ounce fresh lime juice
  • 2 ounces soda water

Instructions:

  1. In a glass, muddle the sugar and mint leaves gently to release the oils.
  2. Add the rum and lime juice, then fill the glass with ice.
  3. Top with soda water and stir gently.
  4. Garnish with a sprig of fresh mint and a lime wedge.
