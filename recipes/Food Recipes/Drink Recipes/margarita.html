Margarita

A margarita is a classic Mexican cocktail that is perfect for warm weather. It's a delicious blend of tequila, triple sec, and lime juice.

Ingredients:

  • 2 ounces tequila
  • 1 ounce triple sec
  • 1 ounce fresh lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Instructions:

  1. In a shaker filled with ice, combine the tequila, triple sec, and lime juice.
  2. Shake well for about 30 seconds.
  3. Strain the mixture into a glass filled with ice.
  4. Garnish with a slice of lime.
